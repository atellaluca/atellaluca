# Luca Atella — Tech Lead, Backend & Platform Engineer

I design and build backends, APIs, and software platforms for products that need to remain understandable, verifiable, and maintainable over time.

At Vemar, from April 2021 to October 2024, I worked as Tech Lead with continuous technical coordination responsibility for a 4-person team. In that context, I personally designed and implemented the infrastructure for 80 trackers and developed the framework on which delis.app is based.

My work tends to focus on boundaries: where application logic ends, where infrastructure begins, how components and services communicate, which dependencies may change, and what effects a change can have on the whole system.

I am based in Basilicata, Italy, and I am open to remote or hybrid collaborations where backend engineering, platform engineering, AWS deployment, runtime validation, or integration-heavy systems are central to the work.

## Method

I approach problems from the operational context first: who uses the system, which process it must support, which parts cannot break, and which technical constraints are already present.

From there I move through diagnosis, boundary definition, explicit contracts, implementation, and verification. The same method appears in modular backends, device infrastructure, AWS platforms, and legacy-environment interventions.

The common thread is governing the relationships between components, data, and environments while considering the effects of changes on the whole system.
